RULES, MODES & THE MELA ENGINE

“Advanced play” here means smarter opponents, richer modes and helpful previews — never rigged dice. Every die is a fair 1-in-6 roll for everyone, and the game proves it in Insights.

The board & a turn
  • 15×15 grid, 52-square loop, 5-square home lane + home: 57 positions per puck (0 = start, 56 = home).
  • Start squares at loop index 0 / 13 / 26 / 39; stars at 8 / 21 / 34 / 47. Starts and stars are safe.
  • Turn order runs clockwise from the roll-off winner (highest single roll; ties re-roll).
  • A 6 brings a puck out (house rule: 1 or 6). A 6, a capture or a puck reaching home gives one more roll.
  • Three 6s in a row: the turn ends and the third move is not played.
  • Landing on a rival on a non-safe square sends it back to its yard (gentle rule: 6 squares back).
  • Exact roll needed for home (house rule off: overshoot counts). No legal move = the turn passes after a 1 s toast.
  • Blockades (house rule): two of your pucks on one square can't be passed or landed on.
  • One legal move (or identical moves) plays itself after 0.4 s — "auto-move" in Settings.

Mela Mayhem — the party mode
  • 4 arrow tiles (loop 3, 16, 29, 42): land exactly and zip 4 squares ahead (can capture at the end).
  • 8 power tiles: draw a power card; hold at most 2; play one before or after your roll.
  • 2 chest tiles: 50–300 coins (shown in the match result, kept only if you finish).
  • Mela events every 5 rounds, announced by a banner, last 2 rounds.
  • Underdog draw: the player in last place draws a card on their next 6 — visible to all, no hidden help.

The Mela engine — how bots think

Every legal move gets a score; personality is a set of weights on the same score:

score = 0.5·run·to
+ 50 (leave yard) + 80 (reach home) + 30 (enter home lane)
+ 18·safe (land on a star) + 90·hunt (capture)
+ 28·safe · (threatbefore − threatafter) + noise

threat = rival pucks 1–6 squares behind that could legally land there next roll (Hard+ also counts 7–12 via 6-then-k at 1/36 each).

Fair dice, proven
  • Random.secure(), uniform 1–6, one roll per press.
  • No rubber-banding, no streak shaping, same for bots.
  • Every roll is logged; Insights shows the 1–6 split against the 1-in-6 line.
  • “Helping dice” is an opt-in house rule, labelled on the board, and never allowed at coin tables or in Wi-Fi rooms unless the host turns it on.
Play helpers
  • Move preview tags: CAPTURE · HOME · SAFE · RISKY on each destination.
  • Hold a puck: path dots, destination ring and the % chance of being hit.
  • Hint (3 per match; unlimited in practice) uses the Hard evaluation.
  • Smart moves %: your choice vs the Expert's, shown after every match.
  • Undo last move in practice only.
Rush & Teams scoring
  • Rush: +1 per square, +20 per capture, −10 when captured, +50 per puck home; 5-minute clock, pucks start out.
  • Ties in Rush: most pucks home, then furthest puck.
  • Teams: partners sit opposite, can share squares (forming a safe block) and never capture each other; when you finish, you roll for your partner.
